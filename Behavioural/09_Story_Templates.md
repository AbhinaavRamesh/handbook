## How to Use This Document

### The STAR Method Refresher

| Component | Purpose | Time Allocation |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **S**ituation | Set the context and background | 15-20% |
| **T**ask | Explain your specific responsibility | 10-15% |
| **A**ction | Detail the specific steps YOU took | 50-60% |
| **R**esult | Share outcomes with metrics when possible | 15-20% |

### Best Practices

1. **Prepare 2-3 versions** of each story at different depths (1 min, 2 min, 5 min)
2. **Use "I" not "we"** - Interviewers want to know YOUR contribution
3. **Quantify everything** - Numbers make stories memorable and credible
4. **Practice out loud** - Stories sound different spoken vs. written
5. **Map to company values** - Tailor emphasis based on company culture

## Adapting Stories for Different Questions

One well-crafted story can answer multiple behavioral questions. Here's how to adapt your stories.

### Story Adaptation Framework

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     YOUR CORE STORY                         │
│                                                             │
│  Same: Situation, basic facts, outcome                      │
│  Changes: Emphasis, framing, which actions you highlight    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                            │
            ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
            ▼               ▼               ▼
     ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐
     │Leadership│    │ Technical│    │ Conflict │
     │  Frame   │    │  Frame   │    │  Frame   │
     └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘
     Emphasize:      Emphasize:      Emphasize:
     - Motivating    - Problem       - Different
       others          solving         viewpoints
     - Vision        - Technical     - Resolution
     - Delegation      decisions       process
```

### Example: One Story, Multiple Questions

**The Core Story**: Led a team to rebuild a failing microservice under tight deadline

| Question Type | How to Adapt |
|---------------|--------------|
| "Tell me about a time you led a team" | Focus on how you motivated the team, made decisions, delegated work |
| "Tell me about a technical challenge" | Focus on the architecture decisions, debugging process, technical tradeoffs |
| "Tell me about working under pressure" | Focus on the timeline, how you prioritized, what you cut |
| "Tell me about a conflict" | Focus on the disagreement about approach and how you resolved it |
| "Tell me about a time you failed" | Focus on early mistakes, what you learned, how you recovered |

### Adaptation Checklist

Before using an adapted story, verify:

- [ ] The story GENUINELY demonstrates the competency being asked about
- [ ] You're not forcing a fit - the adaptation feels natural
- [ ] You have enough detail on the relevant aspects
- [ ] The story is appropriate for the question's scope
- [ ] You haven't already told this story to this interviewer

## Final Tips

### The Night Before

1. Review your story inventory - know which stories you'll use
2. Practice your top 5 stories out loud one more time
3. Review the company's values/principles and map your stories
4. Get a good night's sleep

### During the Interview

1. **Listen carefully** to the exact question being asked
2. **Take a breath** before answering - it's okay to pause
3. **State your story choice** briefly: "I'd like to tell you about when I..."
4. **Watch the interviewer** for cues - are they engaged or checking the time?
5. **End with the result** - don't trail off
6. **Ask if they want more detail** if time permits

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | How to Avoid |
|---------|--------------|
| Too much "we", not enough "I" | Practice saying "I did X, which enabled the team to Y" |
| No metrics in results | Always have at least one number, even if estimated |
| Story doesn't match the question | Pause and pick the right story, don't force fit |
| Too long | Practice timing; aim for 2-3 minutes |
| Too short | Add more detail to your actions section |
| Badmouthing others | Focus on the situation, not the people |
| Vague actions | Use specific verbs: "I analyzed", "I built", "I presented" |

*Remember: The best stories are true, specific, and show both your competence and your character. Good luck!*
