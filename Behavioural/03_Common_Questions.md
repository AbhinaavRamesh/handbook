## 1. Leadership & Influence

### Without Authority
1. **"Tell me about a time you led a project without being the official leader."**
   - Companies: Google, Amazon, Meta
   - Tests: Emergent leadership, influence skills
   - Tip: Emphasize HOW you rallied the team

2. **"Describe a situation where you had to influence stakeholders to adopt your idea."**
   - Companies: Amazon, Microsoft, Google
   - Tests: Persuasion, data-driven advocacy
   - Tip: Show the process, not just the outcome

3. **"Tell me about a time you motivated a struggling team member."**
   - Companies: Google, Meta, Microsoft
   - Tests: Empathy, coaching ability
   - Tip: Focus on understanding their perspective first

### Decision Making
4. **"Describe a difficult decision you made with incomplete information."**
   - Companies: Amazon, Anthropic, Google
   - Tests: Judgment, bias for action
   - Tip: Explain your framework for deciding

5. **"Tell me about a time you made an unpopular decision."**
   - Companies: Google, Amazon, Netflix
   - Tests: Conviction, backbone
   - Tip: Show you listened but stood firm with reasoning

6. **"How did you handle a situation where you disagreed with your manager?"**
   - Companies: All companies
   - Tests: Professional disagreement, respect for hierarchy
   - Tip: Disagree and commit pattern

### Taking Initiative
7. **"Tell me about something you did that wasn't asked of you."**
   - Companies: Amazon, Google, Meta
   - Tests: Ownership, proactivity
   - Tip: Quantify the impact of your initiative

8. **"Describe a time you identified a problem before anyone else."**
   - Companies: Amazon, Anthropic, Stripe
   - Tests: Foresight, ownership mentality
   - Tip: Show how you escalated appropriately

## 2. Teamwork & Collaboration

### Cross-functional Work
9. **"Tell me about a successful cross-functional project you worked on."**
   - Companies: Meta, Google, Microsoft
   - Tests: Collaboration, communication
   - Tip: Highlight how you bridged different perspectives

10. **"Describe how you worked with someone whose style was very different from yours."**
    - Companies: Google, Meta, Microsoft
    - Tests: Adaptability, empathy
    - Tip: Show genuine appreciation for different approaches

### Supporting Others
11. **"Tell me about a time you helped a colleague succeed."**
    - Companies: Google, Microsoft, Amazon
    - Tests: Team player, mentoring
    - Tip: Focus on their success, not your heroism

12. **"Describe a situation where you had to cover for a teammate."**
    - Companies: Meta, Amazon, Microsoft
    - Tests: Reliability, team commitment
    - Tip: Show no resentment, just support

### Building Consensus
13. **"How did you get a group of people to agree on a difficult topic?"**
    - Companies: Google, Amazon, Meta
    - Tests: Facilitation, influence
    - Tip: Describe your process for finding common ground

14. **"Tell me about a time when opposing views led to a positive outcome."**
    - Companies: Google, Anthropic
    - Tests: Cognitive diversity appreciation
    - Tip: Show how conflict was productive

## 3. Conflict Resolution

### Interpersonal Conflict
15. **"Tell me about a conflict you had with a coworker and how you resolved it."**
    - Companies: All companies
    - Tests: Emotional intelligence, professionalism
    - Tip: Own your part in the conflict

16. **"Describe a time you received harsh feedback. How did you respond?"**
    - Companies: Microsoft, Netflix, Meta
    - Tests: Growth mindset, resilience
    - Tip: Show genuine learning, not defensiveness

17. **"How did you handle a situation where a team member wasn't pulling their weight?"**
    - Companies: Amazon, Meta, Google
    - Tests: Direct communication, fairness
    - Tip: Show empathy before accountability

### Technical Disagreements
18. **"Tell me about a time you disagreed with a technical decision."**
    - Companies: Google, Amazon, Anthropic
    - Tests: Technical judgment, professional disagreement
    - Tip: Data-driven advocacy, then commitment

19. **"Describe a situation where you had to change your approach based on feedback."**
    - Companies: All companies
    - Tests: Openness, adaptability
    - Tip: Show genuine consideration of alternatives

## 4. Problem Solving

22. **"How did you approach a problem you'd never seen before?"**
    - Companies: Google, Anthropic, DeepMind
    - Tests: Learning agility, first principles thinking
    - Tip: Show your research and breakdown process

### Under Pressure
23. **"Tell me about a time you had to deliver under a tight deadline."**
    - Companies: Meta, Amazon, Stripe
    - Tests: Time management, prioritization
    - Tip: Show how you scoped and delivered

24. **"Describe a crisis you managed at work."**
    - Companies: Amazon, Google, Meta

## 5. Failure & Learning

### Personal Failures
25. **"Tell me about your biggest professional failure."**
    - Companies: All companies
    - Tests: Self-awareness, growth mindset
    - Tip: Be genuine—interviewers detect fake failures

26. **"Describe a project that didn't go as planned. What did you learn?"**
    - Companies: Microsoft, Google, Amazon
    - Tests: Reflection, accountability
    - Tip: Show concrete behavior changes

27. **"Tell me about a time your work was criticized."**
    - Companies: Netflix, Meta, Google
    - Tests: Handling feedback, improvement
    - Tip: Show you sought to understand, then improved

### Learning from Others
28. **"Describe a time you changed your mind based on new information."**
    - Companies: Amazon, Google, Anthropic
    - Tests: Intellectual humility, adaptability
    - Tip: Give credit to the person/data that changed your view

29. **"Tell me about something you learned from a junior colleague."**
    - Companies: Google, Microsoft
    - Tests: Humility, continuous learning
    - Tip: Show genuine respect for all contributors

## Curveball Questions

40. **"If you could go back, what would you do differently in your career?"**
    - Tests: Self-reflection, honesty
    - Tip: Pick something meaningful, show growth

41. **"What's an opinion you hold that most people disagree with?"**
    - Companies: Google, Anthropic
    - Tests: Independent thinking, conviction
    - Tip: Pick a professional opinion, explain your reasoning

42. **"How would you improve our product?"**
    - Companies: Meta, Google, Apple
    - Tests: Product sense, preparation
    - Tip: Show you've used the product thoughtfully

43. **"What would you do in your first 90 days?"**
    - Tests: Planning, humility
    - Tip: Balance learning with early wins
