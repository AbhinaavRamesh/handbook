## Google's Four Hiring Attributes

Google evaluates candidates on four key attributes:

1. **General Cognitive Ability (GCA)** - Problem-solving and learning ability
2. **Role-Related Knowledge (RRK)** - Technical skills for the position
3. **Leadership** - Emergent leadership and influence without authority
4. **Googleyness** - Cultural fit and collaborative mindset

---

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Prepare 10-15 STAR stories covering diverse themes
- [ ] Practice each story in 2-3 minute format
- [ ] Map stories to Google's key competencies
- [ ] Review common questions and identify which stories fit
- [ ] Practice with a timer (30 sec situation, 60% on actions)
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask your interviewer
