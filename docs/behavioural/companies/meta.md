# Meta - Core Values Interview

Meta's behavioural interview evaluates alignment with their core company values. The interview is typically called the "Core Values" or "Culture Fit" round.

## Meta's Core Values

### 1. Move Fast
- Ship quickly, iterate based on feedback
- Don't let perfect be the enemy of good
- Show examples of rapid execution

### 2. Be Bold
- Take calculated risks
- Challenge the status quo
- Demonstrate ambitious projects

### 3. Focus on Long-Term Impact
- Think about scalability
- Consider future implications
- Show strategic thinking

### 4. Build Awesome Things
- Craft and quality matter
- Technical excellence
- Pride in your work

### 5. Be Open
- Transparent communication
- Share information freely
- Give and receive feedback

### 6. Be Direct and Respect Your Colleagues
- Honest feedback
- Constructive disagreement
- Collaboration over politics

## Meta Interview Structure

| Round | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Behavioural | 45 min | Core values alignment |

## Sample Meta Behavioural Questions

### Move Fast
1. **Tell me about a time you had to deliver under a tight deadline**
   - Show urgency and prioritization
   - Demonstrate shipping mindset

2. **Describe a project where you had to make a decision with incomplete information**
   - Show bias for action
   - Explain risk assessment

### Be Bold
3. **Tell me about a time you took a risk that didn't pay off**
   - Show willingness to take chances
   - Demonstrate learning from failure

4. **Describe when you challenged a decision made by leadership**
   - Show respectful pushback
   - Data-driven argumentation

### Focus on Long-Term Impact
5. **Tell me about a decision you made considering long-term vs short-term tradeoffs**
   - Strategic thinking
   - Balancing priorities

### Build Awesome Things
6. **Describe something you built that you're particularly proud of**
   - Technical depth
   - Attention to detail

### Be Open & Direct
7. **Tell me about a time you received difficult feedback. How did you respond?**
   - Growth mindset
   - Self-awareness

8. **Describe a conflict with a colleague and how you resolved it**
   - Direct communication
   - Collaborative resolution

## Meta-Specific Tips

::: tip Key Phrases
- "I moved fast by..."
- "The long-term impact would be..."
- "I was direct with the team about..."
- "We shipped quickly and iterated..."
:::

::: warning What Meta Looks For
- **Impact at scale** - Meta operates at billions of users
- **Ownership** - End-to-end responsibility
- **Collaboration** - Cross-functional work
- **Speed** - Rapid iteration and learning
:::

## STAR Format for Meta

Meta interviewers particularly value:

| Element | Meta Focus |
|---------|------------|
| **Situation** | Context + scale (users, data size) |
| **Task** | Your specific ownership |
| **Action** | Speed of execution + collaboration |
| **Result** | Quantified impact + learnings |
