# STAR Story Framework

> Build your personal story bank using the STAR method

---

## The STAR Method

Every behavioural answer should follow this structure:

| Element | Purpose | Time Allocation |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **S**ituation | Set the context | 20% (~30 sec) |
| **T**ask | Your specific responsibility | 10% (~15 sec) |
| **A**ction | What YOU did | 50% (~60 sec) |
| **R**esult | Quantified outcome | 20% (~30 sec) |

---

## Story Categories You Need

Prepare at least one story for each category:

| Category | Trait Demonstrated | Example Scenario |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Ambiguity** | Comfort with unclear requirements | Project with vague specs |
| **Failure** | Intellectual humility, learning | Bug you introduced, missed deadline |
| **Initiative** | Bias for action | Problem you fixed proactively |
| **Impact** | Results orientation | Measurable business outcome |
| **Conflict** | Collaboration, communication | Disagreement with colleague |
| **Innovation** | Creative problem-solving | Novel solution you designed |
| **Leadership** | Influence without authority | Led without formal power |
| **Ownership** | End-to-end accountability | Project you fully owned |

## Story Template

Use this template for each story:

### Story: [Give it a memorable name]

**Use For**: [List question types this story answers]

**Traits Demonstrated**: [List 2-3 traits]

---

#### 2-Minute Version

**Situation** (30 sec):
> [Set the context. What was the challenge? Who was involved? What were the stakes?]

**Task** (15 sec):
> [What was YOUR specific responsibility? Make it clear this was your job.]

**Action** (45 sec):
> [What did YOU do? Be specific. Use "I" not "we". Include your decision-making process.]

**Result** (30 sec):
> [Quantified outcome. Numbers matter! What was the impact? What did you learn?]

---

#### 5-Minute Version (Follow-up Details)

**On the context**:
> [Deeper background on why this was challenging]

**On your approach**:
> [Why you chose this path over alternatives]

**On obstacles**:
> [Challenges you faced and how you overcame them]

**On learnings**:
> [What you'd do differently, what you learned]

## Story Quality Checklist

For each story, verify:

- [ ] **Specific**: Contains concrete details, not vague generalities
- [ ] **Measurable**: Includes numbers (%, users, $, time saved)
- [ ] **Your role**: Clear what YOU did vs. the team
- [ ] **Learnings**: Shows growth and reflection
- [ ] **Appropriate length**: Can tell in 2 minutes, expand to 5
- [ ] **Practiced**: Have said it out loud multiple times

## Common Follow-Up Questions

Be prepared for these follow-ups on any story:

| Follow-up | What They're Testing | How to Respond |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------|
| "What would you do differently?" | Self-awareness, growth | Be honest, show reflection |
| "What was the hardest part?" | Problem-solving depth | Pick a genuine challenge |
| "How did others contribute?" | Collaboration, humility | Credit teammates |
| "What did you learn?" | Growth mindset | Specific, non-obvious insight |
| "What happened after?" | Long-term thinking | Show sustained impact |
| "Walk me through your decision" | Structured thinking | Explain trade-offs considered |

## Tips for Strong Stories

### Do This
- Use "I" statements to show your contribution
- Include specific numbers and metrics
- Show your thinking process, not just actions
- Demonstrate learning and growth
- Practice out loud until natural

### Avoid This
- Vague outcomes ("it went well")
- Taking all credit, ignoring team
- Stories where you waited for direction
- Blaming others for failures
- Sounding scripted or robotic

---

## Story Length Adaptation

### 1-Minute Version (for quick responses)
- Situation: 1 sentence
- Action: 2 sentences
- Result: 1 sentence
- Skip Task section

### 2-Minute Version (standard)
- Full STAR as described above

### 5-Minute Version (deep dives)
- Add technical details
- Include alternatives considered
- Discuss obstacles and solutions
- Share lessons learned
- Mention what you'd do differently

---

## Pre-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Have 6-8 stories prepared
- [ ] Each story covers at least 2 traits
- [ ] Can tell each in 2 minutes without notes
- [ ] Have 5-minute details ready for follow-ups
- [ ] Know which story to use for each question type
- [ ] Practiced with at least one other person
- [ ] Feel confident but not robotic
