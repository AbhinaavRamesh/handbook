## How to Use This Document

Each question includes:
- **Why They Ask**: What trait they're evaluating
- **Your Story**: Which STAR story to use
- **Key Points**: What to emphasize
- **Sample Opening**: How to start your answer

Practice saying each answer out loud, adapting to your natural speaking style.

## Handling Unexpected Questions

If you get a question you haven't prepared for:

1. **Pause and think** (5-10 seconds is fine)
2. **Ask clarifying questions** if needed
3. **Pick the closest story** from your bank
4. **Adapt on the fly** — the frameworks still apply
5. **Be honest** if you need a moment: "That's a great question — let me think about the best example..."

**Never say**:
- "I don't have an example of that"
- "That's never happened to me"
- "I can't think of anything"

**Instead say**:
- "Let me think of the most relevant example..."
- "I haven't faced that exact situation, but a related challenge was..."
- "The closest scenario I can think of is..."
