# Common Behavioural Questions

> 25 high-frequency questions organized by category

---

## How to Use This Document

Each question includes:
- **Why They Ask**: What trait they're evaluating
- **What to Cover**: Key points for your answer
- **Red Flags**: What to avoid

Map your own stories to each question type.

---

## Category 1: Ambiguity & Adaptability

### Q1: "Tell me about a time you worked on a project with unclear requirements"

**Why They Ask**: Can you navigate uncertainty?

**What to Cover**:
- How vague the initial direction was
- How you defined the problem space
- Iterative approach with continuous learning
- Successful outcome despite ambiguity

**Red Flags**: Showing frustration, needing clear specs to function

---

### Q2: "Describe a situation where you had to make a decision with incomplete information"

**Why They Ask**: Can you act without perfect data?

**What to Cover**:
- Trade-offs you evaluated
- How you gathered enough signal
- What assumptions you made explicit
- How you validated or adjusted later

**Red Flags**: Analysis paralysis, waiting for perfect information

---

### Q3: "How do you handle changing priorities?"

**Why They Ask**: Are you adaptable?

**What to Cover**:
- Specific example of priorities shifting
- Your pragmatic reaction
- What you did to adapt
- Positive outcome of the pivot

**Red Flags**: Frustration with change, rigidity

---

## Category 2: Leadership & Influence

### Q4: "Tell me about a time you led without formal authority"

**Why They Ask**: Can you influence without position power?

**What to Cover**:
- Situation where you weren't the designated leader
- How you built consensus
- Evidence-based persuasion
- Outcome and relationship preservation

**Red Flags**: Needing authority to lead, steamrolling others

---

### Q5: "Describe a time you influenced a decision you didn't own"

**Why They Ask**: Can you have impact beyond your scope?

**What to Cover**:
- Whose decision it was
- How you provided input constructively
- Data or evidence you brought
- How you accepted the final outcome

**Red Flags**: Overstepping, not respecting boundaries

---

### Q6: "Tell me about a time you mentored someone"

**Why They Ask**: Do you develop others?

**What to Cover**:
- Who you mentored and context
- Your approach to teaching
- How they grew
- What you learned from it

**Red Flags**: Taking credit for their success, not adapting style

---

## Category 3: Failure & Learning

### Q7: "Tell me about a time you failed"

**Why They Ask**: Intellectual humility, learning from mistakes

**What to Cover**:
- A genuine failure (not a humble brag)
- What went wrong and your role
- What you learned
- How you applied that learning

**Red Flags**: Blaming others, never admitting mistakes

---

### Q8: "Describe receiving critical feedback. How did you respond?"

**Why They Ask**: Can you accept feedback gracefully?

**What to Cover**:
- The feedback you received
- Your initial reaction (honest)
- How you processed it
- Changes you made as a result

**Red Flags**: Defensiveness, dismissing feedback

---

### Q9: "Tell me about a time you were wrong"

**Why They Ask**: Do you separate ego from ideas?

**What to Cover**:
- Your incorrect assumption or decision
- How you discovered you were wrong
- How you corrected course
- What you learned

**Red Flags**: Never admitting being wrong, blaming circumstances

---

## Category 4: Technical Achievement

### Q10: "Tell me about your most significant technical achievement"

**Why They Ask**: Depth of impact, technical excellence

**What to Cover**:
- The technical challenge
- Your innovative approach
- Measurable results
- Why it was significant

**Red Flags**: Overstating impact, not explaining technical decisions

---

### Q11: "Describe a complex technical problem you solved"

**Why They Ask**: Problem-solving depth

**What to Cover**:
- Problem complexity and constraints
- Your debugging/analysis approach
- The solution and why it worked
- Impact and learnings

**Red Flags**: Jumping to solutions without analysis, superficial understanding

---

### Q12: "Tell me about a time you raised the bar on quality"

**Why They Ask**: High standards

**What to Cover**:
- Why the current state wasn't good enough
- Your higher standard
- How you achieved it
- Impact of the improvement

**Red Flags**: Accepting mediocrity, "good enough" mentality

---

## Category 5: Collaboration & Conflict

### Q13: "Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict with a teammate"

**Why They Ask**: Conflict resolution skills

**What to Cover**:
- Nature of the disagreement
- How you approached the conversation
- How you found common ground
- Outcome and relationship after

**Red Flags**: Avoiding conflict, escalating unnecessarily, winning at all costs

---

### Q14: "Describe working with a difficult colleague"

**Why They Ask**: Interpersonal skills

**What to Cover**:
- What made them difficult
- Your approach to working with them
- How you maintained productivity
- Any relationship improvement

**Red Flags**: Badmouthing the colleague, not trying to understand them

---

### Q15: "How do you handle disagreements about technical approaches?"

**Why They Ask**: Collaborative problem-solving

**What to Cover**:
- Specific technical disagreement
- How you presented your view
- How you evaluated alternatives
- How the decision was made

**Red Flags**: Being inflexible, not considering other views

---

## Category 6: Impact & Results

### Q16: "Tell me about a project with measurable business impact"

**Why They Ask**: Results orientation

**What to Cover**:
- The business problem
- Your role and approach
- Quantified results
- Sustained impact

**Red Flags**: No numbers, unclear connection to business value

---

### Q17: "Describe a time you exceeded expectations"

**Why They Ask**: Going above and beyond

**What to Cover**:
- What was expected
- What you actually delivered
- How you went beyond
- Impact of the extra effort

**Red Flags**: Just meeting expectations, not understanding what "beyond" means

---

### Q18: "Tell me about a time you delivered under a tight deadline"

**Why They Ask**: Execution under pressure

**What to Cover**:
- The deadline and stakes
- How you prioritized
- Trade-offs you made
- Successful delivery

**Red Flags**: Cutting corners on quality, burning out team

---

## Category 7: Ethics & Values

### Q19: "Tell me about a time you had to make an ethical decision"

**Why They Ask**: Values alignment

**What to Cover**:
- The ethical dilemma
- Your reasoning process
- The decision you made
- Long-term impact

**Red Flags**: Prioritizing metrics over users, not recognizing ethical dimensions

---

### Q20: "Describe pushing back on something you disagreed with"

**Why They Ask**: Courage and integrity

**What to Cover**:
- What you disagreed with and why
- How you raised your concern
- How you handled the outcome
- What you learned

**Red Flags**: Not speaking up, being confrontational

---

### Q21: "Have you ever sacrificed short-term gains for long-term benefits?"

**Why They Ask**: Strategic thinking

**What to Cover**:
- The short-term temptation
- Why you chose long-term
- How you justified the decision
- The eventual outcome

**Red Flags**: Always choosing short-term, not understanding trade-offs

---

## Category 8: Innovation & Creativity

### Q22: "Tell me about a time you solved a problem creatively"

**Why They Ask**: Innovation mindset

**What to Cover**:
- Why standard approaches didn't work
- Your creative solution
- How you validated it
- Results of the innovation

**Red Flags**: Always following the playbook, not questioning assumptions

---

### Q23: "Describe a situation where you challenged the status quo"

**Why They Ask**: Willingness to improve things

**What to Cover**:
- What needed changing
- Your proposed improvement
- How you drove the change
- Impact of the change

**Red Flags**: Change for change's sake, not considering downsides

---

## Category 9: Self-Awareness

### Q24: "What's your biggest weakness?"

**Why They Ask**: Self-awareness, growth mindset

**What to Cover**:
- A genuine weakness (not a humble brag)
- How you've worked to improve
- Progress you've made
- How you manage it now

**Red Flags**: "I work too hard", not being honest, no growth

---

### Q25: "Tell me about yourself"

**Why They Ask**: Communication, self-presentation

**What to Cover**:
- Brief background (1-2 sentences)
- Current role and key achievements
- Why you're here / what you're looking for
- Why you're excited about this opportunity

**Red Flags**: Too long, unfocused, not relevant to role
